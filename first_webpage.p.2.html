One of My Favorite Stories

Jackson, a twelve-year-old boy with ADHD and dyslexia, has struggled in school and been expelled multiple times. During a field trip to the Metropolitan Museum of Art, his math teacher, Mrs. Dodds, transforms into a monster and attacks him. Percy is saved by his Latin teacher, Mr. Brunner, who gives him a magical sword. After defeating Mrs. Dodds, Percy realizes that the world is not as ordinary as it seems, and only his friend Grover, a satyr, remembers the encounter Percy’s mother, Sally Jackson, takes him on a trip to Montauk to escape his abusive stepfather, Smelly Gabe, but they are attacked by a Minotaur. His mother disappears in a golden light, and Percy defeats the monster, obtaining one of its horns as a trophy. Percy is then taken to Camp Half-Blood, a safe haven for demigods, where he learns that he is the son of Poseidon, making him a demigod. At camp, he meets Annabeth Chase, daughter of Athena, and begins to understand his powers and heritage . The central conflict arises when Zeus’s master lightning bolt is stolen, and Poseidon is blamed. Percy, along with Grover and Annabeth, embarks on a cross-country quest to retrieve the bolt and prevent a war among the gods. Their journey takes them through St. Louis, Denver, Las Vegas, and finally to the Underworld in Los Angeles, where they confront Hades, who initially accuses Percy of theft. Percy discovers that the bolt magically appeared in his backpack, revealing that the true thief is Ares, the god of war Percy battles Ares on Santa Monica Beach, defeats him, and recovers the helm of darkness as well. The quest concludes with Percy returning the lightning bolt to Zeus, restoring peace among the gods, and returning to Camp Half-Blood, having grown in confidence, skill, and understanding of his demigod identity. The novel blends Greek mythology with modern adventure, exploring themes of friendship, bravery, identity, and the challenges of growing up as a demigod in a world filled with monsters and gods. It sets the stage for the subsequent books in the Percy Jackson & the Olympians series.

by: Rick Riordan
